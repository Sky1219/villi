# Villi: two-minute demo (phone viewport)

**Setup, before going on stage:**
- Run `npm run seed` once. Never seed during the demo: it competes with live traffic for scheduled jobs.
- On the phone, open the app and tap **See it as my dad**.
- On a laptop (the "second device"), open the app and also tap **See it as my dad**. It's the same anatomy but a separate anonymous profile.
- Have a medicine bottle (or the printed label of metoprolol succinate ER 50 mg) and a sports drink on the table.
- Pre-run one check of each so the FDA label cache is warm.
- Use a personal hotspot, not venue Wi-Fi, and keep a screen recording as a fallback.

| # | Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Home screen | "My dad lost his whole large intestine and most of his small intestine. Nobody told us his extended-release pills might not work in a body like his." |
| 2 | 0:10 | Tap **Take a photo** and photograph the metoprolol ER label | "The photo is read and deleted. Watch the trace: his anatomy, the FDA label and NDC dosage form (`EXTENDED RELEASE`), the guidelines, the community." |
| 2b | 0:35 | Answer lands: **Flag to discuss** | "Every claim is cited: the label, the AGA update. Tap a chip and you see the exact sentence." Point at the gut diagram: "This is *his* gut. The dashed parts are gone, and this design needs time in a gut that is now shorter." Point at the question card: "It never tells him what to do. It writes the one question to bring to the pharmacist." Tap **Add to appointment sheet**. |
| 3 | 0:55 | Follow-up: type **Can I crush it?** | "It quotes the label (swallow whole) and the guideline that crushing is only ever considered for plain immediate-release tablets, and asks the pharmacist." |
| 4 | 1:10 | Photograph (or type) **Gatorade** | "Drinks too. Sports drinks are low in salt and high in sugar for an end-jejunostomy. The swap to ask about is from the community board, clinician-endorsed, with counts for people with his anatomy, marked demo data." |
| 5 | 1:25 | Type **What can I have that's sweet?** | "The safest version of what you want: guideline first, then what people like him logged, then a care-team answer, labeled as another user's report." |
| 6 | 1:35 | On the **laptop**: open the Gatorade answer, tap **Made it worse** | On the phone, the "like you" count ticks up live. "Live Convex queries. And that log was screened by Claude before it counted." |
| 7 | 1:45 | **I saw something** → yellow, oval | "Matched only against his own meds: pantoprazole DR is a possible match. A tablet in the pouch can be undissolved or an empty shell, so show the pharmacist." |
| 8 | 1:55 | **Your sheet** → **Print or save PDF** | "Questions, his own patterns as hypotheses with counts, his meds with what the labels say. This is what he brings Monday." |

**If something breaks:**
- Answer still "Checking": the reviewer is slow. Keep talking about the trace; it's the point.
- "AI service is busy": tap **Try again**, or switch to the screen recording.
- Photo misread: tap **Not right? Type the name instead**.
